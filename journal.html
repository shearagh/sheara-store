Ingredients & rituals

A closer look at the ingredients behind Sheara and the simple routines they support.

Our hero ingredient

Why shea butter matters.

Shea butter is naturally rich and emollient, making it a beautiful ingredient for body care. It helps soften the feel of dry skin and leaves a comfortable, nourished finish.

Sheara Lotion

Why we chose niacinamide.

Niacinamide, also known as vitamin B3, is widely used in skincare to support the skin barrier and improve the appearance of uneven tone and texture. We included it in our Lotion as part of a thoughtful daily formula.

Raw ingredient

Sunflower oil for softness.

Sunflower oil contains fatty acids and vitamin E and is used in skincare for its conditioning, moisturizing feel. In Sheara, it complements the richer character of shea butter.

Antioxidant care

The role of vitamin E.

Vitamin E is a well-known antioxidant used in cosmetic formulations. It also contributes to the conditioning feel of a nourishing body-care formula.

Body ritual

Moisturize while skin is still comfortable.

After bathing, gently pat your skin dry and apply your moisturizer. Massage it in slowly, paying attention to areas that tend to feel dry.
