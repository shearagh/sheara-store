The goodness behind Sheara

Nature's ingredients.
Real benefits.

We choose ingredients for the role they play in caring for your skin — from the richness of shea butter to the skin-conditioning benefits of niacinamide.

01

Shea Butter

Rich and naturally emollient, shea butter helps soften and moisturize the skin while supporting a comfortable, nourished feel.

02

Niacinamide

Featured in our Sheara Lotion, niacinamide is a form of vitamin B3 commonly used to support the skin barrier and improve the look of uneven tone and texture.

03

Sunflower Oil

A plant oil rich in fatty acids and vitamin E that helps condition the skin and leave it feeling soft and moisturized.

04

Vitamin E

An antioxidant used in skincare formulations to help condition the skin and complement a nourishing body-care routine.

Why shea butter?

Nature's richness,
made for your ritual.

Shea butter has a long history of use in African communities as a rich skin-conditioning ingredient. At Sheara, it sits at the heart of our body-care story because of its luxurious feel and ability to leave skin feeling nourished, soft and cared for.
