Behind the brand

About Sheara

A Ghanaian body-care brand inspired by the nourishing richness of shea butter.

Our philosophy

Nourishment you can feel.

Sheara was created around a simple idea: body care should feel nourishing, uncomplicated and beautiful. We create products for everyday routines, with shea butter at the heart of our story. We also choose supporting ingredients with a clear purpose, including niacinamide, sunflower oil and vitamin E in our Lotion.

From our Glow Body Cream to our Lotion, Sheara is about giving your skin a little more care in the moments that belong to you.
